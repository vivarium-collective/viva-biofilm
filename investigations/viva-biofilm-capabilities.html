🧬 Biology — the mechanism this investigation models A biofilm begins as a handful of cells attached to a surface. As they grow and divide, they consume the solutes diffusing in from the bulk liquid faster than diffusion can…

A biofilm begins as a handful of cells attached to a surface. As they grow and divide, they consume the solutes diffusing in from the bulk liquid faster than diffusion can replenish them near the substratum, so a concentration gradient forms: richer at the boundary, poorer at the base. That gradient in turn shapes where growth is fastest, producing the characteristic spatially structured, substrate-limited colony this investigation demonstrates and visualizes.

🔬 Scientific argument 3 for · 0 against viva-biofilm grows a spatially structured, substrate-limited biofilm from a small inoculum using fixed (non-tuned) parameters, in seconds, with…

Main claim. viva-biofilm grows a spatially structured, substrate-limited biofilm from a small inoculum using fixed (non-tuned) parameters, in seconds, with reproducible (seeded) determinism — and this spatial capability composes with process-bigraph the same way the well-mixed chemostat path does.

Evidence for

  • spatial-biofilm-growth: 40 -> 640 agents, 12.1 um thickness, substratum/boundary substrate ratio 0.49 (a clear substrate-limitation gradient), from one fixed, deterministic run (this study).
  • runtime-and-scaling: ~303,000 agent-steps/sec peak throughput; grid, population, and duration scaling curves all produced with no configuration exceeding the 30s slow threshold (this study).
  • composability: a BoundaryControllerProcess drove the oxygen boundary on schedule (0.5 g/m^3 during t=1d-3d, restored to 8.74 after, matching reference exactly) and the biofilm's growth visibly responded -- 60 vs. 120 final agents, biomass ratio 0.574 vs. an identically-seeded un-perturbed control (this study).

Caveats

  • IbM is stochastic; results are single-seed demonstrations of qualitative behavior, not statistical ensembles.
  • PDE/relaxation/detachment are simplified vs iDynoMiCS's exact algorithms (see viva-biofilm-equivalence for the well-mixed quantitative equivalence claim).
  • Duration scaling is near- but not exactly linear (R^2=0.907): population growth via division during longer runs compounds per-step cost.
